Each game flashes a single instruction and gives you a few seconds to do it. Every five games, everything speeds up.

How to play

  • Read the wordPOP IT, SWAT IT, FLIP IT. The instruction tells you what to do.
  • Beat the fuseEach game lasts a few seconds. The fuse at the bottom shows how long is left.
  • Four livesMiss a game and you lose a heart. Lose all four and the run ends.
  • Speed upEvery five games is a stage. Each stage is faster than the last.
  • Tilt and shakeSome games use the phone itself: tilt to steer, shake to shake. Touch works too.

Common questions

My score isn’t on the leaderboard.
The leaderboard uses Game Center. Check you’re signed in under Settings → Game Center, then finish another run.
There’s no sound.
Check the speaker button on the main menu, and that your iPhone isn’t on silent. Micro Mayhem follows the silent switch.
I can’t feel the vibrations.
Check the vibration button on the main menu, and that System Haptics is on under Settings → Sounds & Haptics.
Tilting doesn’t move anything.
Hold the phone upright and tilt it left or right. You can always drag with your finger instead.
My best score disappeared.
Your best score is saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app clears it, but your Game Center leaderboard score stays.
