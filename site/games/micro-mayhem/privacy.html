Bishimitsu / Games / Micro Mayhem / Privacy

Micro Mayhem is made by Luke Bishop, an individual developer based in the United Kingdom. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

In short: Micro Mayhem does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your best score and your sound and vibration settings are saved on your device only. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Motion sensors

A few games let you tilt or shake your iPhone. The game reads the motion sensors only while one of those games is on screen, uses the readings straight away to move things in the game, and does not store or send them anywhere.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your score to Apple so it can appear on the leaderboard. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy, which you can read at apple.com/legal/privacy. We cannot see your Apple Account details. You can turn off Game Center at any time in Settings.

Children

Micro Mayhem is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
